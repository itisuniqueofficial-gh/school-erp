# USER_GUIDE.md — End-User Manual

> Screenshots are indicated with _[screenshot: …]_ placeholders.

## 1. Logging in
1. Go to `https://your-school-domain/login`. _[screenshot: login page]_
2. Enter your **12-digit User ID, email, or username** and **password**,
   optionally tick *Remember me*.
3. Click **Sign in**. You are taken to the dashboard for your role.
- Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and follow
  the reset link. _[screenshot: forgot password]_
- After 5 failed attempts your account is temporarily locked for security.

## 2. The dashboard
Shows KPI cards (students, teachers, staff, classes), fee collection, today's
attendance %, fee-trend and students-by-class charts, recent activity and
notices. _[screenshot: dashboard]_

Use the **sidebar** to open modules and the **top bar** for search, the
notification bell, and your profile menu (Change password / Sign out). On mobile,
tap the ☰ icon to open the sidebar.

## 3. Students
- **Students → list**: search, filter by class/section/status, paginate. _[screenshot: students list]_
- **Admit Student**: fill personal, academic and guardian details; optionally
  create a student login and upload a photo. Admission numbers are generated
  automatically. _[screenshot: admit student]_
- **Profile**: view details, guardians and documents; **Edit** to update.
- **Bulk promote**: select students and choose a destination class.

## 4. Teachers & staff
- **Teachers**: add/edit teaching staff, view qualifications and subject allocations.
- **Staff**: manage non-teaching staff (inline add form).

## 5. Academics
Tabs for **Years, Classes, Sections, Subjects**. Add records with the side form.
Mark one academic year as *current*.

## 6. Attendance
1. **Attendance**: choose class, section and date → **Load students**.
2. Set each student's status (or use *All present* / *All absent*) → **Save**.
3. **Monthly report**: pick class/section/month for a day-by-day grid (P/A/L/…). _[screenshot: attendance]_

## 7. Examinations
- Create exams; **Enter marks** per class & subject (grades auto-assigned).
- **Report cards**: ranked results with pass/fail; printable & exportable.

## 8. Fees
- **Collect fees**: search a student, see invoices, click **Pay**, choose method
  (cash, cheque, UPI, bank transfer, or an enabled online gateway) — a receipt is
  generated. _[screenshot: fee collection]_
- Online payments (Razorpay / PhonePe / MobiKwik, if enabled by the admin) are
  verified on the server before a payment is marked successful.
- **Structures**: define fee structures per class.
- **Receipt**: printable payment receipt.

## 9. Library
Add books, **Issue** to a student with a due date, and **Return** (overdue fines
calculated automatically). _[screenshot: library]_

## 10. Transport & Communication
- **Transport**: manage routes, vehicles/drivers, and assign students to routes.
- **Communication**: publish **notices** (targeted audiences) and **broadcast
  messages** (in-app notification + email to all users).

## 11. Reports
Open **Reports**, pick a report (attendance, fees, academic, students, staff),
view on screen, then **Export CSV** or **Print**.

## 12. Settings
Edit the school profile and system settings; trigger a **database backup**.

## 13. Backups
**Backups**: create, download, restore or delete database backups. _Restoring
overwrites current data — download first._

## 14. Your account
Open **My Profile** from the top-right menu to view your 12-digit User ID and
**upload/replace your profile picture** (JPG/PNG/WebP). Use **Change password**
to set a strong password (8+ chars with upper, lower case and a number).

## Portals
- **Student portal**: attendance %, fees due, results, timetable.
- **Teacher portal**: today's schedule, classes, attendance marking.
- **Parent portal**: children overview and their fees.
